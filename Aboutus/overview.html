Introduction

With a mission to extend World Class healthcare solutions to the community through advances in medical technology, medical research and by adopting best man management practices - Synergy Superspeciality Hospital has launched its newly built flagship hospital with NABH accreditation in Rajkot. The hospital complex is located in the posh western suburb of Rajkot, just 20 minutes from the airport and in close vicinity of most hotels and shopping malls. With vast open spaces around the hospital it's positioned & designed to ensure that patients and caregivers get a feel of the skyline of Rajkot city from their rooms and never feel claustrophobic. It's an attempt to give a feel of home @ Synergy Superspeciality Hospital . Synergy Superspeciality Hospital is a 130 bed Super speciality medical institution spanning 5 floors and 2 basement floors. This world class & highly sophisticated medical establishment offers all major medical specialities, subspecialities, investigation & diagnostics facility, rehabilitation & physical therapy care under one roof.
