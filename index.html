Our Mission

With a mission to extend World Class healthcare solutions to the community through advances in medical technology, medical research and by adopting best man management practices - Synergy Superspeciality Hospital has launched its newly built flagship hospital in Rajkot.

It gives us immense pleasure to introduce Synergy Superspeciality Hospital, Rajkot as one of the largest Superspeciality Hospitals in Saurashtra & Kutch region. It has tertiary care facilities like Anaesthetists, Cardiology & Cardiovascular and Thoracic Surgery, Neurosurgery & Neurology, Trauma & Joint Replacement Surgery, Critical Care Unit, GI Surgery & Gastroenterology, Oncology, Orthopaedic, Pathologist, Radiologist etc.
